# Rep Coach privacy policy

_Last updated 22 October 2026_

Rep Coach is a personal strength-training tracker. This policy explains what data it uses and where that data goes.

## Data the app uses

- **Workouts you log or import**: exercises, sets, weights, reps and effort ratings, plus a CSV export from Hevy if you choose to import one.
- **Health Connect data, only if you allow it**: sleep sessions, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, heart rate and steps. The app only reads this data. It never writes to Health Connect.
- **Settings you enter**: your Claude API key, your choice of model, and any notes for the coach.

## Where it is stored

Everything is stored on your phone, in the app's private storage. There is no Rep Coach server or account, and the developer never receives your data.

## What leaves your phone

When you ask for a session plan and have added a Claude API key, the app sends a summary to Anthropic's Claude API using your own key. The summary contains your last 14 days of workouts, lift trends, weekly volume, today's energy and time, your notes, and that day's recovery figures (sleep hours, resting heart rate, HRV and steps). Anthropic handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal). Nothing is sent if you don't add a key.

Health Connect data is used only to adjust your training plan. It is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

## Your control

- You can turn off Health Connect access at any time in Android's Health Connect settings.
- Removing your API key stops all data from being sent.
- "Save a backup" exports your workouts to a file you choose. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.
