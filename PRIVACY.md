# Rep Coach privacy policy

_Last updated 26 October 2026_

Rep Coach is a personal strength-training tracker. This policy explains what data it uses and where that data goes.

## Data the app uses

- **Workouts you log or import**: exercises, sets, weights, reps and effort ratings, plus a CSV export from Hevy if you choose to import one.
- **Settings you enter**: your Claude API key, your choice of model, any notes for the coach, and your Hevy API key if you turn on Hevy sync.

## Where it is stored

Everything is stored on your phone, in the app's private storage. There is no Rep Coach server or account, and the developer never receives your data.

## What leaves your phone

When you ask for a session plan and have added a Claude API key, the app sends a summary to Anthropic's Claude API using your own key. The summary contains your last 14 days of workouts, lift trends, weekly volume, today's energy and time, and your notes. Anthropic handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal). Nothing is sent if you don't add a key.

If you add a Hevy API key, the app reads your workouts from Hevy and, when you tap Send to Hevy, writes that plan to your Hevy account as a routine. Hevy handles that data under its own terms.

Your data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

## Your control

- Removing your Claude API key stops data being sent to Claude; removing your Hevy key stops Hevy sync.
- "Save a backup" exports your workouts to a file you choose. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.
